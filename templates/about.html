System Overview & Methodology

JOBPOST AGGREGATOR

"Search. Discover. Apply."

1. Mission & Vision

In today's fragmented hiring market, job seekers must constantly tab across dozens of isolated career portals, company boards, and regional listings. This leads to duplicate applications, expired opportunities, and scattered tracking.

JobPost Aggregator is a centralized career discovery and aggregation platform. We collect, organize, deduplicate, filter, and index authentic job posts from authorized job sources across the globe into a single unified search experience.

2. Core Aggregation Architecture

📡

Multi-Source Feed Ingestion

Continuous synchronization connectors gather data from TechCareers, RemoteHub, CampusHire, DevJobs, and partner feeds.

🔍

Smart Duplicate Clustering

Cross-source deduplication clusters matching roles side-by-side, preserving source integrity while preventing repetitive candidate spam.

🛡️

Direct Source Authentication

Candidates always apply directly on the legitimate employer or partner portal. Zero hidden proxy forms or resume brokering.

📊

Personal Application Pipeline

Candidates can log and track interview progress across 5 stages (Applied, Reviewing, Interviewing, Offered, Rejected) in real time.

3. Technology Stack

Built strictly adhering to the specified technical constraints with maximum performance and zero bloated frameworks:

  • Frontend: Pure Semantic HTML5, Modular CSS3 Architecture (custom tokens, flexbox & grid), and Vanilla ECMAScript.
  • Backend: Python 3.11 with Flask framework & RESTful routing endpoints.
  • Database: Relational MySQL/MariaDB with indexed schema, foreign keys, and normalized entity design.
  • No Heavy Frameworks: Strictly zero React, Vue, Angular, Node.js, Bootstrap, or Tailwind dependencies.
