How JobPost Aggregator Works

One unified search engine connecting multiple authorized job boards with duplicate detection and verified application links.

🔍

2. Smart Deduplication & Expiry

Cross-board posts are automatically clustered so you never see repetitive spam. Expired deadlines are continuously detected and pruned.
